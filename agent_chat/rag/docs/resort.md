# ResortEase's Facilities Information
## Description
This documents is contains information about the facilities.

## Rules
- Observe cleanliness. Keep the resort clean and throw garbage in the proper bins.
- Follow swimming pool rules. No running, pushing, or rough playing around the pool.
- Wear proper swimming attire. Use appropriate swimwear in the swimming pool.
- Take care of resort property. Do not damage furniture, decorations, plants, or equipment
- Observe safety precautions. Follow lifeguard instructions and posted safety signs.
- Avoid excessive noise. Keep music and voices at a reasonable volume, especially at night.
- No diving in shallow areas. Dive only in designated areas where it is permitted.
- Supervise children. Parents or guardians must watch their children at all times.
- No littering. Do not leave trash around the pool, beach, cottages, or rooms.
- Follow food and drink policies. Observe rules about bringing outside food and beverages.
- Respect other guests. Be polite, avoid disturbing others, and respect their privacy.
- No smoking in prohibited areas. Smoke only in designated smoking areas.
- Follow resort staff instructions. Cooperate with employees and security personnel.
- Observe resort opening hours. Use facilities only during the permitted hours.
- Guests must check out at the scheduled time stated in their booking or by the property. Late check-out may incur an extra fee, depending on how many hours the guest stays beyond the standard check-out time.
## Requirements for a Guests before check-in
The person who's reserve/book the party is the one who's responsible for the party.
- ResortEase Account – The person responsible for the party is required to have a ResortEase account.
- Payment – Settle any remaining balance, or deposit.
- Check-in time – Arrive during the property's designated check-in hours. Early check-in may require approval or an additional fee
- Special requirements – Inform the property in advance about extra guests, children, pets, or special requests, if applicable.
- Property rules – Follow the accommodation's policies regarding occupancy limits, smoking, visitors, and other house rules.

## Accommodation
- Free internet connection through wireless (Wi-Fi) or wired connection.
- Pet are allowed but it is required to have diapers
- We have towels, toiletries and air conditioning. (They are not free)
- We provide blankets and other assets though they may charge extra fee.

## Payments
If you wish to pay your reservation deposit or settle your balance, please contact the branch directly through its phone number or email, or visit our branches to make your payment in person.
A 50% deposit is required to confirm your reservation. We recommend making your payment as soon as possible to secure your preferred date, as slots may be taken by other guests.
We accept GCash, MariBank, and credit cards as payment methods.

## Reservation and Booking
You can make a reservation through our website or by visiting our branch, as we also accept walk-in guests.

You can check if your preferred date is available by visiting our website or contacting us at our physical location.

To check the status of your reservation, you can log in to your account on our website or check your email for updates.

Guests are allowed to cancel their reservations. If the guest has not paid yet, they can simply leave the reservation as it is. However, if the guest has already paid, go to the website, navigate to Reservations page, and cancel the booking to process the deposit refund.

Please note that the refund is not full; a 50% deduction will apply.

Guests are not allowed to transfer their reservations to another person.

Additional guests are subject to the capacity limits and applicable fees of your selected accommodation or package.
